## Official source review

Official pages checked on 2026-11-13:

- [HRD Korea EPS-TOPIK test description](https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/abot/exam/selectTopikDesc.do?lang=en): lists 40 questions in 50 minutes, with Reading and Listening at 20 questions/25 minutes each; it describes relative selection and industry-specific successful-candidate criteria.
- [HRD Korea testing criteria](https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/abot/exam/selectStandardDesc.do?lang=en): describes practical information, signs and occupational safety in Reading, and numbers, visual materials and conversations in Listening.
- [HRD Korea standard textbook catalog](https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/book/std/standardBookList.do?lang=en): lists NEW standard textbooks, edition 2024, and related audio; the page notes some changes to Textbook 2 in June 2026 and marks the material KOGL Type 3, attribution/no modifications.
- [HRD Korea official notices](https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/cust/aid/NoticeList.do?lang=en): lists the June 22, 2026 notice about updates to NEW Standard Korean Textbook 2.
- [HRD Korea public Reading samples](https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/book/pub/publicBookReading.do?lang=en) and [Listening samples](https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr/epstopik/book/pub/publicBookListening.do?lang=en): retained as external source links only.

The notice and catalog confirm that a partial Textbook 2 update was announced. The linked HWP/ZIP files were not downloaded or compared page-by-page, so this release does not claim to summarize the exact amendments or reproduce their contents. Public sample catalogs are not described as actual recent exam papers. No protected questions, diagrams, audio or textbook pages are copied or rehosted. Synthetic browser speech is labeled as such and is not official exam audio or pronunciation scoring.

## Simulator integration

Preparation lesson links use the existing guided practice modes. The lesson page preselects the requested Reading, Listening, vocabulary/grammar, everyday, workplace/safety or incorrect-question mode. The existing simulator, immutable published versions, timers, resumable attempts and scoring remain in place. Results map the current skill categories (`vocabulary`, `grammar`, `everyday`, `workplace`, `safety`) to related preparation lessons. Unknown future skill names are shown as unmapped, without fabricated recommendations. If a mapped lesson is still locked, the result links to that module's course location and explains the 40-lesson prerequisite. EPS exam access remains independent of all 50 curriculum checkpoints.

## Qualified teacher review checklist

Passing software tests does not establish native-level Korean quality or exam equivalence. A qualified Korean-language teacher should review:

- spelling, spacing, idiomatic phrasing and politeness across all ten new lessons;
- number systems, counters, dates, time expressions and item-to-explanation alignment;
- safety command wording and whether Thai explanations avoid overclaiming operational instructions;
- each checkpoint for one unambiguous answer, plausible distractors and accurate Thai rationale;
- Listening prompts for natural rhythm and appropriate beginner difficulty, including how browser TTS voices realize them;
- the course-to-skill mapping after future simulator skill categories change.
