## Resource behavior

Every Everyday Korean lesson links to its attributed Thai MOOC course page, with lesson-specific topic notes. The date lesson also links the Online King Sejong Institute curriculum, and the review lesson links HRD Korea's official EPS-TOPIK book listing. These are external educational pages, not videos. We did not find and verify exact, freely accessible Thai-language video pages for all ten topics during this pass, so the player labels these resources as online lessons and does not claim video coverage. Learners can use browser Korean speech synthesis in the listening section when their device provides a Korean voice. The synthesized speech is an optional aid, not a human recording or pronunciation score.
