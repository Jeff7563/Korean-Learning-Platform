# V0.7.2 Teacher Tips and Mini Lessons

## Editorial approach

The lesson player contains five optional, original Thai-authored mini lessons. They appear after the main lesson notes and before the existing activity checklist. Each includes a clear objective, concise explanation, Korean examples with Thai translations, a practice reminder, and three original multiple-choice questions with explanations. Mini-lesson responses are temporary UI state; they do not create learner records, mark activities complete, affect checkpoints, or unlock lessons.

The assessment tip distinguishes this app’s original practice from official EPS-TOPIK tests and other platforms. It does not name or link the unverified KHEM KOREA demonstration.

## Review limitation

The mini-lessons include basic grammar and example sentences, but automated integrity checks do not replace review by a qualified Korean-language teacher. In particular, learners should receive teacher review of context-sensitive contrasts such as 은/는 versus 이/가 and naturalness/register across everyday situations.
