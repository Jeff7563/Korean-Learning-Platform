# EPS-TOPIK official source audit

Audit date: 2026-11-12. The application links learners to the official sources; it does not import official questions, audio, or textbook files. Direct downloads are linked by HRD Korea as HWP/ZIP files. The browser audit could identify their links and listed contents, but could not decode/download those binaries, so their bytes, internal page contents, and exact audio tracks have not been inspected.

HRD Korea's guide identifies EPS-TOPIK as a 40-question, 50-minute test (20 Reading, 20 Listening) with four-choice questions. It states that since 2013 the exam has been based on the standard textbooks and is 100% confidential. The official 2025 public question book is sample material for format familiarization and study, not an authenticated 2025 actual exam. It must not be described as a recent past paper. This is EPS-TOPIK, not ordinary TOPIK I/II.

## Copyright boundary

HRDKorea's copyright policy says its website content is protected, unauthorized copying/distribution is generally prohibited, and material changes are not allowed without permission. A public download is not automatically reusable as an interactive question bank. KOGL Type 3 permits attribution-based use without modifications; it does not authorize adapting textbook material into rewritten questions. Therefore the simulator contains original mock items only, kept separately from these external source records.

The mock sets use the official exam's published shape as a study format, not its protected questions, diagrams, scripts, or audio. They are labeled **ORIGINAL MOCK**. Browser speech synthesis is synthetic practice audio and is never attributed to HRD Korea or presented as official listening audio.

## Current ingestion status

- Official HTML catalogs and the HRDKorea copyright policy were reviewed on the audit date.
- HWP and ZIP links were visible, but no downloaded binary was decoded or ingested. Exact pages, audio tracks, answer correctness, and detailed permissions for those downloads remain unverified.
- All official content remains external. Contact HRDKorea for permission before redistribution, adaptation, or commercial use.
